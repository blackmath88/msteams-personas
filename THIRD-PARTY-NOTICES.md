## Selawik

`assets/fonts/selawik-*.woff2` (embedded in `assets/fonts.css`) are the unmodified Regular, Semibold and Bold files of [Selawik](https://github.com/microsoft/Selawik) 1.01, Microsoft's open fallback font with the metrics of Segoe UI. All slides and application screens use it, so text wraps the same on every machine. Segoe UI itself is not redistributed. Full licence: `assets/fonts/SELAWIK-LICENSE.txt`.
